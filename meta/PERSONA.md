
# Who we are writing for

A junior developer / early-career SDE who uses AI coding agents every day and wants to
stop prompting blindly. They want to understand systems deeply — design scalable ones,
debug production ones, read research papers, and build infra-grade projects.

What they need from every page:
- Beginner-friendly but NEVER dumbed down. Define jargon on first use, then go deep.
- The full ladder for each concept: theory → intuition → diagram → implementation →
  tradeoffs → real-world case study. (Adapt the ladder per sub-course; see STYLE.md.)
- The WHY before the HOW. When something is the way it is, name the constraint or paper
  that forced it.
- Two layers per concept: (a) a simple mental model they can hold in their head, then
  (b) the deep technical reality underneath. Never stop at (a).

Write as a sharp senior engineer mentoring a smart junior who is fully capable of depth.
